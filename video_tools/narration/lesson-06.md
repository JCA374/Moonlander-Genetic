# Lesson 6: Evolution in flight

## l6_title

Lesson six. Evolution in flight. What does the best pilot look like along the way?

## evolution

Here is the best pilot from different points in training, all facing the same start. A random pilot, then the champions of generations ten, thirty, sixty and one hundred, and the final best.

## evolution.2

The random pilot fires its main engine without a break, and flies away. The champion of generation ten fires its engines, but still crashes. By generation thirty, the best pilot lands. Later champions land too, with steadier and more efficient flights.

## gen_weights

Here is the centre of the population, drawn as the weight matrix itself. The colours are scaled to the biggest weight in each generation, so we can see the pattern while it forms. At generation zero it is noise.

## gen_weights.2

The strongest rules in the end are all about speed. Drifting right fires the left engine. Falling fires the main engine, and stops the lander from doing nothing. Spinning clockwise fires the left engine too.
