# Lesson 6: Evolution in flight

## l6_title

Lesson six. Evolution in flight. What does the best pilot look like along the way?

## evolution

Here is the best pilot from different points in training, all facing the same start. A random pilot, then the champions of generations ten, thirty, sixty and one hundred, and the final best.

## evolution.2

The random pilot fires its main engine without a break, and flies away. The champion of generation ten fires its engines, but still crashes. From generation thirty on, every champion lands.

## gen_weights

Here is the centre of the population, drawn as the weight matrix itself. The colours are scaled to the biggest weight in each generation, so we can see the pattern while it forms. At generation zero it is noise.

## gen_weights.2

The strongest rules in the end are about tilt, spin and drift. Tilting left or spinning left fires the right engine. Drifting right fires the left engine. And the leg switches, which only matter at the very end, tell it to go quiet.
